SECTION 02

2. Critical Distinction You Must Know (Vesna vs. Vodyanitsa & 4★ Check)

You listed Vesna (Vesta) and Vodyanitsa side by side, but you must understand their exact mechanical difference so you don't brick your party composition.
Passive Talent: "Starlight Blessing: Vernal Swirl"

Possesses the innate passive "Starlight Blessing: Vernal Swirl", allowing her to convert standard Cryo Swirl into "Stellar Swirl" all by herself.

(Because Vesna herself is an Anemo character, simply pairing her with 3 Cryo characters like Qiqi, Escoffier, or Shenhe completes the Vesna (Anemo) + Cryo combo and triggers Stellar Swirl non-stop.)

Vesna (Anemo) + 3 Cryo Example Teammates (Instant Stellar Swirl)
Warning: Has NO Conversion Trigger (No Starlight Blessing)!

Warning: Vodyanitsa does NOT have a conversion trigger (Starlight Blessing) to turn regular Swirl into Stellar Swirl on her own!

Instead, through her passive "Final Cantor", she is a dedicated amplifier support who takes the "Stellar Vortex" already created by a teammate (Vesna, Odette, Sandrone, Cryo Traveler, The Tsaritsa, etc.) and upgrades it into a 2nd-stage "Drifting Vortex" while shredding Anemo RES by 35%.

Therefore, if you slot only Vodyanitsa and remove Vesna / Odette / Sandrone / Cryo Traveler / The Tsaritsa, Stellar Swirl will never activate at all.

4★ Fact-Check Q. Are there any 4-Star characters that convert reactions into Stellar or Lunar reactions?

No! There is not a single 4-star character that can independently convert standard Elemental Reactions into Stellar or Lunar reactions!
(Nod-Krai Lunar reactions strictly require pulling a limited 5-star character, while for Snezhnaya Stellar reactions, the only free-to-play conversion trigger is the "Cryo Traveler".)
The four regional 4-star dedicated supports below work just like Vodyanitsa: they cannot convert reactions on their own and only buff/support Special Reactions already enabled by a 5-star trigger (or Cryo Traveler).
